How ordering works

From your order to your door.

  1. Request access

    Fill in the form. We set up your business account and send you the app.

  2. Order in the app

    Pick a category, choose what you need, how much and the delivery day.

  3. Pay in the app

    Pay for your order in the app. We then arrange pickup from the farms.

  4. We collect and cool

    Each lot is weighed and graded at the farm and goes into a cold truck within hours of picking.

  5. Track and sign for it

    Follow the truck on a map. Check the temperature record and sign for your order on arrival.

Questions buyers ask

When can we start ordering?

The app launches November 1, 2026 so you can set up your account first. First deliveries are planned for December 2026.

What can we order?

Fruit, vegetables, meat, fish, lamb, eggs, dairy and beverages, all from day one.

How do we pay?

In the app, when you place your order.

Can we place a large standing order?

Yes. Tell us your weekly needs in the form, for example two tons of tomatoes a week, and we will plan the farmers and the trucks around it.

Can we see that the food stayed cold?

Yes. Our trucks and cold containers log their temperature continuously, and you can check the record when you sign for your order.

We are outside Ghana. Can we order?

Yes. Fill in the buyer form, choose "Buyer outside Ghana" and tell us your country, what you need and how much. We will contact you to plan your first order.
