How to download

It takes a minute. Search for CryoChain and look for our logo of four leaves around a snowflake.

On Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your phone.
  2. Search for CryoChain.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. Open the app and choose whether you are a farmer or a buyer. Follow the short tutorial.
QR code for Google Play. Add it on launch day.

On iPhone

  1. Open the App Store on your phone.
  2. Search for CryoChain.
  3. Tap Get.
  4. Open the app and choose whether you are a farmer or a buyer. Follow the short tutorial.
QR code for the App Store. Add it on launch day.

What the app does

For farmers

  • Sign up and verify yourself, with a short tutorial.
  • Set up your mobile money payout.
  • See orders you can fill, with the price and pickup day.
  • See your weight, your grade and your payment.
  • Take part of your payment as fertilizer if you choose.
  • Keeps your entries when there is no signal.

For buyers

  • Browse fruit, vegetables, meat, fish, lamb, eggs, dairy and beverages.
  • Order and pay in the app.
  • Follow the truck on a map.
  • Check the temperature record on delivery.
  • See which farm your food came from.
  • Track orders from inside or outside Ghana.

For booking a cold truck

  • Book by the hour, the day or a regular run.
  • Set pickup, drop off and temperature.
  • Follow the truck live on the map.
  • Get the temperature record when the job is done.
  • Rent space in the cold store.

No smartphone?

Farmers can still sell with us from any phone. We send a text message when the truck is coming and when you have been paid.

Or contact us and we will help you get started.
