How it works for you

You can sign up yourself, or our agent can help you. Either way, every step is recorded on your own account.

  1. Sign up

    Register in the app, or fill in the form on this page and we will call you. A short tutorial shows you how to set up your mobile money payout.

  2. Get an order

    When a buyer orders a crop you grow, we offer you a share of it, with the price and the pickup day.

  3. We collect and weigh

    Our agent weighs and grades your crop at your farm and takes a photo. You see the weight and the grade.

  4. It goes cold

    Your crop goes straight into the cold truck, then on to the cold store or the buyer.

  5. You get paid

    Mobile money within 24 hours. Take part of it as fertilizer or other supplies if you choose.

Sell together, get paid separately

One order is often shared by several farmers, collected from each farm gate or from a shared village collection point. Each farmer is still weighed, graded and paid on their own, so no one waits for anyone else.

We take fruit, vegetables, eggs, dairy, meat, fish and lamb.

Questions farmers ask

How do I know the price?

We tell you the price before the truck comes, so you can decide before you pick.

When do I get paid?

By mobile money, within 24 hours of your delivery being confirmed.

Do I need a smartphone?

No. The app works on Android phones and iPhones, and on a basic phone we text you when the truck is coming and when you have been paid.

Do I need internet all the time?

No. The app and our agents' phones save everything when there is no signal and send it when the phone finds signal again.

Is the fertilizer a loan?

No. We do not lend anything. After you deliver and your payment is due, you can choose to take part of it as supplies like fertilizer, ordered in the app. Nothing is given before you deliver, and there is no interest or repayment.

Where do you collect?

At your farm gate, or a shared village collection point. We are starting in a few parts of Ghana and growing. Put your town and region in the form and we will tell you when pickup reaches you.
