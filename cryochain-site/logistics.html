Book it your way

Three ways to book a truck, plus space in our cold store. All in the app.

By the hour

Quick runs that need to stay cold. You choose the start time and how many hours.

Good for: market pickups, kitchen top ups, fish from the landing, moving stock between shops.

By the day

The truck comes to you and stays on site, working as a cold room on wheels until the event is over.

Good for: weddings, funerals, festivals, conferences and catering jobs.

Regular runs

The same route on the same days, every week or every day. Set it once and we keep it running.

Good for: mining camps, hotels, supermarkets and exporters.

How a booking works, start to finish

What happens before, during and after your job.

  1. Before the day

    You book in the app or send the form. We confirm the date, the hours and the temperature, and send you the truck and driver details.

  2. The truck arrives cold

    The cold box is brought down to your temperature before it reaches you, so your goods go straight into the cold.

  3. During the job

    You follow the truck live. If the temperature moves, the driver and our team are alerted in the same minute, while there is still time to fix it.

  4. After the job

    You get the temperature record for the whole booking, ready to show your own customers.

Planning an event?

Tell us these five things and we can confirm your truck quickly.

  • The date, and the hours you need the truck on site.
  • The venue address, and where the truck can park.
  • What you are keeping cold, and about how much.
  • Chilled, frozen, or both.
  • The name and phone number of the person meeting the truck.

Space in our cold store

When your own cold room is full, broken down, or you simply have nowhere cold to put it.

  • Cold room or freezer broken down. Keep your stock cold with us until it is fixed.
  • Harvest surge. Store the extra instead of selling it cheap.
  • Too few buyers this week. Hold it cold and sell when prices recover.
  • Ship, port or paperwork delays. Goods wait in the cold, not on the dock.
  • Truck breakdown or road closure. Goods wait cold until the route is clear.

Our cold store keeps running through a full day without mains power, so a blackout never warms your goods.

Questions about booking

Can I book a truck for just a few hours?

Yes. Choose By the hour in the app or the form, and tell us the date, the hours, and the pickup and drop off.

Can the truck stay at my event all day?

Yes. Book By the day and the truck stays on site, keeping food and drinks cold until you are done.

Can I book for several days in a row?

Yes. Choose By the day and tell us every date you need in the form, or set up regular runs.

Does a driver come with the truck?

Yes. A trained CryoChain driver comes with every truck. Tell us if you also need help loading and unloading.

How much can a truck carry?

Up to 1.1 tons a load, chilled or frozen. For more, book more runs or more hours.

How do I know the price?

You see it in the app before you confirm. If you use the form, we send the price with your booking confirmation.

Can I change my booking?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will confirm the change.

Can I store goods with you instead?

Yes. Rent space in our cold store, held at a set temperature until your goods move on.

Can I see the temperature after the job?

Yes. Every truck logs its temperature the whole time, and you get the record for your booking.

Where do you operate?

We are starting in a few parts of Ghana and growing. Put your pickup and drop off in the form and we will confirm.

When can I book?

Send a booking request now and we will hold your date. First runs are planned for December 2026, and the app launches November 1, 2026.
